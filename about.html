Our Story

Sustained Safety & Continuity

At FRS VIP Protection, we understand that security is more than just a service — it's a commitment to your safety, peace of mind, and business continuity. We specialise in commercial guarding, armed response, VIP protection, VIP transportation, and technical security installations, ensuring that you, your assets, and your people are always protected.

Founded in 2022 by Shirven Sukhu, FRS VIP Protection was established with a vision to provide reliable, high-quality security solutions in a competitive market. We saw the need for a security company that combines robust security protocols with modern technology and rigorous training, setting a new standard for excellence.

Our services cater to commercial clients who demand quality security solutions. Whether you require ongoing security contracts or specialised VIP protection, we are committed to building long-term partnerships based on trust and reliability.
